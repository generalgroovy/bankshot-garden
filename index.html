Bankshot Garden

Turn mirrors to send one seed through every flower.

Mirror rules

The seed moves straight until it meets a mirror. Each mirror makes a right-angle turn. A flower blooms when the seed crosses its tile. Bloom every flower in one launch.

The path stays visible so you can trace it back. Change any mirror to try again. Undo edit restores your previous arrangement and its last shot, including after Reset garden. There is no timer or attempt limit. Use Tab and Enter or Space to turn mirrors with a keyboard.
